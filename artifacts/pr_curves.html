At the chosen operating point the two are tied

Identical false-positive counts on test — 8 each. BLASTp catches one more true positive, so its precision denominator is 48 rather than 47; the 0.004 difference is arithmetic, not a difference in error behaviour. The separation is in the curve, not at this point.

Thresholds were selected on validation and applied to test unchanged: probe P(polyester) ≥ 0.1455, BLASTp e-value ≤ 3.16e-2. The BLASTp database holds only the 303 training positives. Dashed line is prevalence, the AU-PRC a random ranker achieves — 0.361 on validation, 0.404 on test. Test drew harder negatives than validation (8 of 62 clear BLASTp's cutoff versus 2 of 76), so part of the gap reflects split composition; on validation the methods are within 0.006 AU-PRC of each other.
